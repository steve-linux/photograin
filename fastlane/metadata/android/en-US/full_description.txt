Photograin is a free photo editor built for photographers: every adjustment is previewed in real time on the GPU, and your original is never touched.

<b>Non-destructive</b>
Edits are saved as a recipe next to the photo, not burned into it. Reopen a photo any time and keep refining, with unlimited undo and redo.

<b>Adjustments</b>
• Light: exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks
• Color: temperature, tint, vibrance, saturation
• Detail: sharpening, clarity, dehaze
• Effects: vignette, realistic film grain
• Crop with aspect ratio presets, straighten, rotate and flip

<b>Compare</b>
Press and hold the photo to see the original, or use the split view.

<b>Full-quality export</b>
Exports are rendered tile by tile at full resolution, to JPEG, PNG or WebP. Location data is removed by default.

<b>Private by design</b>
No permissions, no network access, no ads, no tracking. Photos are opened and saved through the system dialogs.
