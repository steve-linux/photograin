Photograin is a free photo editor built for photographers: every adjustment is previewed in real time on the GPU, and your original is never touched.

<b>Non-destructive</b>
Edits are saved as a recipe next to the photo, not burned into it. Reopen a photo any time and keep refining, with unlimited undo and redo.

<b>Adjustments</b>
• Light: exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks
• Color: temperature, tint, vibrance, saturation
• Detail: sharpening, clarity, dehaze
• Effects: vignette, realistic film grain
• Styles: ready-made presets, your own presets, and .cube LUTs with adjustable strength

<b>Crop and geometry</b>
Aspect ratio presets, rotate and flip, straighten by hand or automatically from the lines in the photo, and perspective correction with sliders or by marking four corners of something rectangular.

<b>Retouch</b>
Heal and clone to remove spots, dust and small objects, and fix red eye with one tap per pupil.

<b>RAW and wide color</b>
Opens DNG, CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, ORF, RW2 and other RAW files with LibRaw, keeping 16 bits per channel all the way to the GPU. Photos with wide-gamut profiles (Display P3, Adobe RGB) keep their most saturated colors.

<b>Batch editing</b>
Apply a preset or copied adjustments to many photos at once and export them all to a folder.

<b>Full-quality export</b>
Exports are rendered tile by tile at full resolution, to JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, JPEG XL or 16-bit TIFF, in sRGB, Display P3 or Adobe RGB. Choose which EXIF data to keep; location is removed by default. Optional 2× or 4× upscaling, bicubic or with AI (Real-ESRGAN, run on the device).

<b>Compare</b>
Press and hold the photo to see the original, or use the split view.

<b>Private by design</b>
No permissions, no network access, no ads, no tracking. Photos are opened and saved through the system dialogs, and the AI model is part of the app.
